# Song Sound

Paste song lyrics and see how they sound, spelled for your readers.

- Lyrics: Chinese (characters or pinyin), Japanese (kana, kanji, romaji), Korean, English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (PT / BR), Finnish, Albanian, Serbian.
- Transcription for readers of Russian, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese.
- Works offline and installs as an app (PWA).
